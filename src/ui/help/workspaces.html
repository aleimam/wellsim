Data boundaries

Workspaces

A workspace is the ownership and authorization boundary around wells, reservoirs, cases, files and exports.

Your private workspace

Every enrolled user has exactly one personal workspace. It cannot invite members. Use it for private studies, learning and work that has not been assigned to a company.

Company workspaces

A company workspace has explicit members and roles. Owners and administrators manage membership. Engineering managers, engineers, reviewers and viewers receive only the permissions assigned to their role.

Selection is not authority

The workspace selector tells WellSim which workspace you want to use. The server still verifies your active membership and permission for every protected request. Knowing or changing a workspace identifier does not grant access.

Before saving: confirm the active workspace. Data created in a company workspace is company data; data in your personal workspace stays personal.

Leaving a company

Leaving ends future access to that company. It does not delete company records and does not affect your private workspace. A company must always retain at least one active owner.
