Organization controls

Companies

Company owners control visibility and privileged roles. Administrators manage ordinary membership without gaining platform-wide authority.

Roles

  • Owner: full company administration, including owner and administrator roles.
  • Administrator: team and company settings, except protected owner/administrator changes.
  • Engineering manager: broad engineering and approval workflow access.
  • Engineer: create, run and export engineering work.
  • Reviewer: read and review without engineering modification rights.
  • Viewer: read-only access.

Private by default

New and existing organizations use invite_only. They are absent from the company directory until an owner or administrator changes the policy to Accept membership requests and supplies a public directory summary.

Two ways to add people

Private invitations are bound to the invited verified email and expire. Directory requests are initiated by a signed-in user and grant no access until approved. Approved requests always begin with the Engineer role; an owner can later change it using the team console.

Company management and request review require recent MFA. WellSim rechecks that assurance on the server; a browser field cannot claim it.

Platform administrators

A WellSim platform administrator is separate from every company role. Company owners cannot publish global help or manage another company, and platform status does not silently create company membership.
