Orientation

Getting started

Choose where your work belongs before entering engineering data. That single choice keeps private and company information correctly separated.

Start in four steps

  1. Open My portal. Sign in with a verified email. Your first verified sign-in creates one private workspace.
  2. Choose a workspace. Use Personal for work owned only by you, or select a company you have already joined.
  3. Open Engineering tools. Select the calculation module and enter inputs with their displayed units.
  4. Review before relying on results. Read assumptions and warnings, then export the suitable evidence for your workflow.

Personal or company?

Personal work is visible only to you. Company work belongs to that organization and follows your assigned role. Joining a company does not move or expose your existing personal work.

A join request is only a request. It creates no membership and no access to company data.

Current availability

The calculation tools remain public. Saving, company collaboration and managed help content appear only when secure sign-in and the portal service are enabled on this deployment.
