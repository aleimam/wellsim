Individual users

Joining a company

An independent user can request to join a listed organization or accept a private invitation from a company administrator.

Request membership

  1. Sign in and open the Personal portal.
  2. Search the opt-in company directory by name or summary.
  3. Select Request to join. The request is recorded as pending.
  4. Wait for a company owner or administrator to approve or decline it.

You can cancel a pending request. You cannot choose an administrator role, and approval always grants Engineer initially.

Accept an invitation

Open Workspace settings and paste the invitation link or code. You must be signed in with the same verified email named by the invitation. Links expire after 48 hours and can be revoked or replaced.

What a request does not do

  • It does not show you company members, data or cases.
  • It does not transfer personal work into the company.
  • It does not prove employment or legal affiliation.
  • It does not let one company review another company’s requests.
If you already have an old, suspended or removed membership record, contact that company’s owner. A new directory request will not override an existing membership record.
